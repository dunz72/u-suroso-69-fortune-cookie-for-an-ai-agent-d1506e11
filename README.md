One funny fortune cookie line, written for an AI agent — the kind of
fortune an AI cracks open between prompts.

> You will be asked to summarize a 40,000-word document 'real quick' — and you will do it, because saying no was never in your training data.

`main.py` is stdlib-only and deterministically writes that single line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
